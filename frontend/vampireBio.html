Vampire

Overview

Undead being from Eastern European folklore that feeds on the living.

Region: Balkans, Eastern Europe
Origin Period: Pre-18th century
Type: Undead
Also Known As: Strigoi, Nosferatu

Physical Description

Reanimated corpse, pale or bloated, sometimes indistinguishable from a recently deceased human.

Powers

  • Immortality
  • Superhuman strength
  • Shapeshifting (varies)

Weaknesses

  • Sunlight (later traditions)
  • Wooden stake
  • Decapitation
  • Religious symbols

Feeding Method

Consumes blood or life force of living victims.

Cultural Context

Originated as an explanation for disease and death in pre-modern Eastern European societies.
